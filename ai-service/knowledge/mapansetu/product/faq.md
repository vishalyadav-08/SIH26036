# MapanSetu FAQ

## Q: What is MapanSetu?
A: It is an integrated online platform for the Legal Metrology Department to digitize processes like application tracking, verification, and certification.

## Q: How do businesses register?
A: Through the main portal under the Business User role.

## Q: What happens if an inspector has no internet?
A: The MapanSetu Field App supports offline syncing, storing inspection data locally until connection is restored.
