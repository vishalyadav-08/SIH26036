# MapanSetu Product Overview

MapanSetu is a digital platform designed to modernize and streamline the certification, inspection, and application processes for Legal Metrology. It allows businesses to register, apply for instrument verification, and receive digital certificates.

## User Roles
1. **Business**: Can register, submit verification applications for their instruments, and view application statuses and certificates.
2. **Inspector/Legal Metrology Officer**: Can view assigned applications, perform physical inspections using the MapanSetu Field App, record observations, and recommend or reject certification.
3. **Admin**: Manages user roles, system configurations, and monitors overall system health.
4. **Public User**: Can verify the authenticity of a certificate using the Public Verification page by entering a certificate ID or scanning a QR code.

## Key Workflows
* **Application Lifecycle**: A business submits an application. It is assigned to an Officer. The Officer conducts an inspection. If approved, a digital certificate is issued. If rejected, the business is notified.
* **Offline Sync**: The MapanSetu Field App allows Inspectors to conduct inspections in areas with poor connectivity. Observations are saved locally and synchronize automatically when the device reconnects to the internet. Conflict resolution is handled by prompting the Inspector if local data conflicts with server updates.
