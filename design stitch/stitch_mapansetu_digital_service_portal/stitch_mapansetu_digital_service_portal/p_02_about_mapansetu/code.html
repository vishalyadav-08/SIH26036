About MapanSetu

Comprehensive coordination platform for Legal Metrology standardization and compliance.

account_balance About the Platform

MapanSetu is an integrated digital ecosystem designed to streamline and transparentize the operations of the Department of Legal Metrology. It acts as the central hub connecting businesses, inspectors, and administrative bodies to ensure standard weights and measures compliance across the state.

By digitizing legacy processes, MapanSetu aims to reduce bureaucratic friction, enhance accountability, and provide a single source of truth for all metrological certifications and compliance records.

hub What MapanSetu Coordinates

The platform orchestrates a multitude of critical functions essential for maintaining metrological integrity:

storefront

Business Registration

Onboarding and profiling of manufacturers, dealers, and repairers.

scale

Instrument Records

Centralized database of all registered weights, measures, and instruments.

app_registration

Applications

Processing requests for new licenses, renewals, and model approvals.

assignment_ind

Inspector Assignment

Algorithmic and manual routing of inspections to authorized personnel.

calendar_month

Scheduling

Calendar management for verification camps and routine inspections.

fact_check

Evidence & Certificates

Digital issuance and verification of compliance certificates.

warning Problem Being Addressed

Prior to MapanSetu, the coordination of legal metrology activities relied heavily on manual record-keeping, decentralized databases, and physical document routing. This led to systemic inefficiencies:

  • Prolonged turnaround times for certificate issuance and renewals.
  • Difficulty in tracking the lifecycle of specific measuring instruments.
  • Lack of real-time visibility into inspection schedules and outcomes for administrators.
  • Challenges for businesses in accessing their compliance status and historical records.

MapanSetu resolves these issues by replacing siloed operations with a unified, transparent digital workflow.

visibility Vision

To establish a transparent, efficient, and technologically advanced Legal Metrology administration that ensures consumer protection and promotes fair trade practices through seamless digital governance.

block Platform Limitations

What MapanSetu Does NOT Do:

  • cancel Does not conduct automated remote calibration of physical instruments.
  • cancel Does not replace the statutory requirement for physical verification by authorized inspectors.
  • cancel Does not handle consumer grievance redressal outside the scope of weights and measures violations.
