General Questions

MapanSetu is the official digital portal for the Department of Legal Metrology. It provides a centralized platform for businesses and citizens to register instruments, verify certificates, track applications, and ensure compliance with standardized measurement regulations across the state.
To verify a certificate, navigate to the "Verify Certificate" section from the main menu. You will need to enter the unique Certificate Number provided on your physical or digital copy. The system will then query the official database and display the current status and validity of the document.
Any individual or business entity that manufactures, repairs, sells, or uses weighing and measuring instruments for commercial transactions must register on MapanSetu to obtain the necessary licenses and schedule periodic calibrations as mandated by law.
The portal integrates with the centralized payment gateway, allowing you to pay processing and licensing fees using Net Banking, major Credit/Debit Cards, and supported UPI applications. All transactions are secure and instantly receipted.
