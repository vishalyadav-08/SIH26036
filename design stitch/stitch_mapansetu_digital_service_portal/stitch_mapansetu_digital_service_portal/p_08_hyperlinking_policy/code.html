We can not guarantee that these links will work all the time and we have no control over availability of linked pages.

Linking to MapanSetu

We do not object to you linking directly to the information that is hosted on this site and no prior permission is required for the same. However, we would like you to inform us about any links provided to this Portal so that you can be informed of any changes or updations therein.

Framing Policy

Also, we do not permit our pages to be loaded into frames on your site. The pages belonging to this site must load into a newly opened browser window of the User.

Prohibited Links

Under no circumstances will MapanSetu establish a link to a website that:

  • Promotes or depicts illegal activities or explicit content.
  • Provides misleading information or engages in fraudulent practices.
  • Violates intellectual property rights.
  • Compromises the security or integrity of the MapanSetu platform.
