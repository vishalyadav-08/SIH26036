Material featured on this site is subject to government copyright protection unless otherwise indicated. The copyright protected material may be reproduced free of charge in any format or media without requiring specific permission. This is subject to the material being reproduced accurately and not being used in a derogatory manner or in a misleading context.

Where the material is being published or issued to others, the source must be prominently acknowledged. However, the permission to reproduce this material does not extend to any material on this site, which is explicitly identified as being the copyright of a third party. Authorization to reproduce such material must be obtained from the copyright holders concerned.

verified_user 2. Permitted Use

Users are permitted to use the content provided on the MapanSetu portal for personal, educational, and non-commercial purposes, provided that such use maintains the integrity of the data and acknowledges the Department of Legal Metrology as the source.

  • Viewing and downloading certificates for verification purposes.
  • Printing pages for personal reference or record-keeping.
  • Sharing links to public educational resources provided on the portal.

content_copy 3. Reproduction Guidelines

Any reproduction of the official content, including but not limited to guidelines, legal texts, and institutional frameworks, must adhere to the following strictly:

  • Accuracy: The content must not be altered, modified, or presented out of context in a way that misrepresents the original intent of the Department of Legal Metrology.
  • Attribution: Clear attribution must be given stating "Source: MapanSetu Portal, Department of Legal Metrology, Government of India."
