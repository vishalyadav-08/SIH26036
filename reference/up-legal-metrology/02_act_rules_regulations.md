# Legal Metrology Act 2009 — Rules & Regulations Summary
## Source: Central Government of India + UP State Implementation

---

## Legal Metrology Act, 2009 — Structure

The Act comprises **57 sections** in **5 Chapters**:

### Chapter I: Preliminary
- Title, extent, commencement
- Definitions of key terms (weight, measure, dealer, manufacturer, repairer, etc.)

### Chapter II: Standard Weights and Measures
- Base units based on the International System of Units (SI)
- Standard specifications for weights and measures

### Chapter III: Appointment and Powers of Officials
- **Section 13-14**: Director of Legal Metrology (Central), Controller of Legal Metrology (State)
- **Section 15-18**: Legal Metrology Officers — powers of entry, search, seizure
- **Section 19-22**: Licensing and Registration Requirements
- **Section 23**: Mandatory license for Manufacturer, Dealer, Repairer

### Chapter IV: Verification and Stamping
- **Section 24**: Every weight or measure used in trade must be verified and stamped
- Re-verification required periodically (typically every 24 months)
- Re-verification mandatory after any repair or dismantling

### Chapter V: Offences and Penalties (Sections 25-57)
- Operating without license — fines + imprisonment for repeat offences
- Non-standard weights/measures — penalties
- Many offences compoundable

## License Categories

### 1. Manufacturer License (Form LM-1)
**Who needs it:** Anyone manufacturing weights and/or measures
**Requirements:**
- Proof of business registration (Incorporation Certificate, Partnership Deed)
- Address proof for business premises
- Identity proof (PAN, Aadhaar)
- **Model Approval Certificate** from Central Government
- Technical specifications of products
- Infrastructure details

### 2. Dealer License (Form LD-1)
**Who needs it:** Anyone trading, selling, or distributing weights and measures
**Requirements:**
- Proof of business registration
- Address proof for business premises (Rent/Lease/Ownership)
- Identity proof of proprietor/partners/directors
- Trade License from local municipal body
- GST Certificate

### 3. Repairer License (Form LR-1)
**Who needs it:** Anyone servicing, calibrating, or repairing weights and measures
**Requirements:**
- Proof of business registration
- Address proof for business premises
- Identity proof
- **Qualification certificates / Technical competence proof**
- Tools and equipment details

## Registration for Pre-Packaged Commodities
### Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011

**Who must register:** Every manufacturer, packer, or importer of pre-packaged commodities

**Rule 27 Requirements:**
- Must register within **90 days** of commencing business
- Base government fee: ₹500 (varies by state: ₹1,000 - ₹5,000)

**Documents Required:**
1. PAN Card of proprietor/partner/company
2. Certificate of Registration/Incorporation
3. Partnership Deed / MoA & AoA (as applicable)
4. Proof of premises ownership / Rent-Lease Agreement
5. Trade License
6. GST Certificate
7. Import Export Code (IEC) — for importers
8. List of items to be packed with packaging sizes
9. List of Directors/Partners
10. Sample product labels/packaging

**Mandatory Label Declarations:**
- Name and address of manufacturer/packer/importer
- Net quantity
- Month/Year of packing
- MRP (inclusive of all taxes)
- Customer care details

## Verification Process

### Initial Verification
1. Instrument manufactured or imported
2. Application submitted with instrument details
3. Legal Metrology Officer conducts inspection
4. Accuracy tested against standards
5. If passed → Stamped with verification mark + validity period
6. Verification Certificate issued

### Re-verification (Periodic)
- Required every **24 months** (varies by instrument type)
- Mandatory after any repair/dismantling
- Same process as initial verification
- New stamp applied

### Verification at Office vs Camp
- **Office Verification:** Business brings instruments to LM office
- **Camp Verification:** LM officer visits business premises on scheduled dates
- Both follow same testing standards

## Fee Structure (State-Specific — UP)
Fees vary by:
- Category of business (Manufacturer/Dealer/Repairer)
- Type and capacity of instruments
- New license vs. renewal
- State government fee schedule

*Note: Specific fee schedules are prescribed by State Legal Metrology (Enforcement) Rules*

## Compliance Calendar
| Activity | Frequency |
|---|---|
| License Renewal | Annually (1 year) or as per state rules |
| Instrument Re-verification | Every 24 months (varies by type) |
| Periodical Returns Submission | Annually / Half-yearly (by role) |
| Pre-packaged Registration | Within 90 days of business start |

## Penalties Summary
| Offence | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Operating without license | Fine + imprisonment (repeat) |
| Using non-standard weights | Fine |
| Failure to get instruments verified | Fine |
| Tampering with stamped instruments | Fine + imprisonment |
| Wrong labeling on packaged goods | Fine + seizure |
| Not filing periodical returns | Fine |
