## Service 1: Manufacturer License

- **Business Category:** Manufacturer of weights, measures, weighing and measuring instruments
- **Statutory Rule:** Section 23 of The Legal Metrology Act, 2009 + Rule 11 of UP LM (Enforcement) Rules, 2011
- **Application Form:** Form LM-1 (Schedule II)
- **Grant of License:** Form LM-2 (Schedule III)
- **Renewal Form:** Form LM-3
- **Competent Authority:** Controller of Legal Metrology, UP
- **Timeline (SLA):** 45 Days (Fast-tracked to 30 days under EoDB)
- **Required Documents:**
  1. Proof of legal possession of factory premises (Sale Deed / Lease / Rent Agreement)
  2. Site map and layout plan of manufacturing premises
  3. Factory License / Udyam Registration (MSME) / Industrial License
  4. Constitution of Firm (Partnership Deed, MOA/AOA, Certificate of Incorporation, GST, PAN)
  5. List of manufacturing machinery, testing facilities, and tools
  6. List of qualified technical staff
  7. **Model Approval Certificate** from Central Government (Section 22)
  8. NOC from local municipal authority / UP Pollution Control Board
  9. Affidavit of non-conviction under Legal Metrology Act

## Service 2: Dealer License

- **Business Category:** Dealer (Wholesalers, retailers, distributors of weighing/measuring instruments)
- **Statutory Rule:** Section 23 + Rule 11
- **Application Form:** Form LD-1 (Schedule II)
- **Grant of License:** Form LD-2 (Schedule III)
- **Renewal Form:** Form LD-3
- **Competent Authority:** Assistant Controller of Legal Metrology (ACLM)
- **Timeline (SLA):** 30 Days (21 days under EoDB)
- **Required Documents:**
  1. Proof of premises (Ownership / Lease / Rent Agreement)
  2. GST Registration Certificate & PAN Card
  3. Shop and Commercial Establishment Registration (UP Shop Act)
  4. Manufacturer's authorization letter / dealership agreement
  5. Partnership Deed / Incorporation Documents
  6. Affidavit regarding compliance with model approval standards

## Service 3: Repairer License

- **Business Category:** Repairer (servicing, adjusting, or repairing weights and measures)
- **Statutory Rule:** Section 23 + Rule 11
- **Application Form:** Form LR-1 (Schedule II)
- **Grant of License:** Form LR-2 (Schedule III)
- **Renewal Form:** Form LR-3
- **Competent Authority:** Assistant Controller of Legal Metrology (ACLM)
- **Timeline (SLA):** 30 Days (21 days under EoDB)
- **Required Documents:**
  1. Premises proof (workshop space specifications)
  2. Proof of technical qualification / ITI / diploma / experience certificate
  3. List of repair equipment, standard test weights, calibration standards
  4. GST Registration & PAN
  5. Affidavit of non-involvement in adulteration/tampering

## Service 4: Verification and Stamping

- **Statutory Rule:** Section 24 + Rules 14-17
- **Certificate Issued:** Verification Certificate (Schedule VIII)
- **Process:**
  1. Person using weight/measure in commercial transactions submits for verification
  2. Verification at Inspector's office/laboratory OR on-site (for fixed/heavy machinery)
  3. Accuracy tested against working standards
  4. Stamping: Verification seal (lead seal, tamper-proof sticker, date punch) with quarter and year
  5. Verification Certificate issued

### Re-verification Periodicity:
| Instrument Type | Period |
|---|---|
| Standard weights, brass weights, capacity measures, length measures, counter scales | Every 24 months |
| Electronic weighing scales, platform scales, spring balances, weighbridges | Every 12 months |
| Petrol/diesel dispensing units, flow meters, automatic rail weighbridges | Every 12 months |
| Bulk oil storage tanks, storage vat calibration | Every 6 months |

## Service 5: Tank Lorry / Storage Tank Calibration

- **Equipment:** Petroleum road tankers, milk tankers, chemical tankers, underground fuel storage tanks
- **Procedure:** Volumetric calibration using working standard measures and proving tanks
- **Certificate:** Tank Calibration Certificate (valid 12 months)

## Service 6: Pre-Packaged Commodity Registration (Rule 27)

- **Business Category:** Packer / Manufacturer / Importer of pre-packaged commodities
- **Statutory Rule:** Rule 27 of The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011
- **Timeline:** 30 Days (15 days under EoDB)
- **Registration Fee:** ₹500 base (varies by state: ₹1,000 - ₹5,000)
- **Must register within 90 days** of commencing business

### Mandatory Label Declarations:
1. Name and address of manufacturer / packer / importer
2. Common or generic name of commodity
3. Net quantity in standard units
4. Month and year of manufacture / packing / import
5. Maximum Retail Price (MRP) inclusive of all taxes
6. Consumer care contact details
7. Country of origin (for imports)
8. Dimensions of container (where relevant)

---

## Service 7: Company Director Nomination (Section 49)

- **Statutory Rule:** Section 49(2) + Rule 29
- **Form:** Form A under Rule 29
- **Timeline:** 30 Days (15 days under EoDB)
- **Purpose:** Companies nominate one Director/Manager responsible for LM compliance
