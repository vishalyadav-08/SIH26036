## Organizational Hierarchy

```
Controller of Legal Metrology (CLM) — State Head / HoD (IAS / Senior Administrative Cadre)
├── Additional Controller of Legal Metrology — State HQ
├── Joint Controller of Legal Metrology (JCLM) — Zonal / HQ oversight
│   ├── Deputy Controller of Legal Metrology (DCLM) — Regional / Division
│   │   ├── Assistant Controller of Legal Metrology (ACLM) — Division / Multi-District
│   │   │   ├── Senior Legal Metrology Officer (SLMO) — Field enforcement, supervisory
│   │   │   │   ├── Legal Metrology Officer (LMO) / Inspector — Ground level
│   │   │   │   │   - Verification, testing, stamping
│   │   │   │   │   - Periodic re-verification
│   │   │   │   │   - Raid and seizure
│   │   │   │   │   - Compounding of minor offences
```

## Metrological Infrastructure

### Standard Laboratories Hierarchy:
1. **Reference Standard Laboratories** — National level (NPL / RRSL)
2. **Secondary Standard Laboratories** — State HQ (Lucknow) and designated regional zones
3. **Working Standard Laboratories** — District and divisional levels

## RTI Structure (Section 4(1)(b) Points)

### Public Information Officers:
- **First Appellate Authority (FAA):** Joint Controller of LM, UP HQ, Lucknow
- **Central PIO (HQ):** Deputy/Assistant Controller (HQ), Lucknow
- **Divisional/District PIOs:** Respective Assistant Controllers / Senior LMOs

### Grievance Escalation:
1. **Level 1 (Field):** Legal Metrology Officer / Inspector
2. **Level 2 (District/Division):** Assistant Controller (ACLM)
3. **Level 3 (Regional):** Deputy/Joint Controller
4. **Level 4 (State):** Controller of Legal Metrology, Lucknow
5. **Government Level:** Principal Secretary, Dept of Food & Civil Supplies

## KPI Metrics Monitored

1. **Verification & Stamping Performance** — Target vs actual, FPS/mandi coverage
2. **Revenue Realization** — Verification fees, licensing fees
3. **Enforcement & Market Surveillance** — Inspections, raids, short-delivery cases
4. **Offence Disposal** — Compounding under Section 48, court prosecutions
5. **EoDB Service Delivery** — % processed within SLA, zero-pendency rate, avg TAT
6. **Citizen Grievance Resolution** — Complaints via IGRS, resolution rate within 30 days
