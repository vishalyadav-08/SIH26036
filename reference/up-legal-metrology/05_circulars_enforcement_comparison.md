## Key Circulars & Administrative Directives

### 1. Nivesh Mitra Single Window Integration
- Mandatory online-only processing for all licenses (Manufacturer, Dealer, Repairer)
- Elimination of physical paper applications
- All renewals processed through digital portal

### 2. Citizen's Charter & Janhit Guarantee Timelines
- Strict adherence to statutory SLAs
- Deemed approval / automated escalation if not processed within mandated timeframes
- No subjective queries allowed

### 3. Central Inspection System (CIS) Reforms (BRAP Compliance)
- Joint inspections between Labour, Legal Metrology, and UP Pollution Control Board
- Prohibition of arbitrary manual inspections
- **Algorithmic, randomized inspection scheduling**
- Risk-based classification: Low / Medium / High Risk
- Low-risk: exempt from routine physical inspections (self-certification)
- **48-hour mandatory upload** of inspection reports

### 4. Multi-Year Licensing
- 1 to 5 years (up to 10 years) in single application
- Reduces annual compliance burden

### 5. Seasonal Enforcement Drives
- Agricultural Mandis: Moisture meters and scales during Rabi/Kharif procurement
- Sugar Mills: Weighbridge surveillance for sugarcane delivery
- Festive Seasons: Sweets, bullion/gold, packaged gift hampers (Diwali)

### 6. Compounding of Minor Offences (SOP)
- Section 48 compounding without court trials
- Uniform penalty slabs across all districts

## Multi-State Legal Metrology Portal Comparison

### National Portal
- **eMaap Portal:** https://emaap.gov.in — Centralized platform by Dept of Consumer Affairs

### State Portals

| State | Portal | Key Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Uttar Pradesh** | legalmetrology-up.gov.in | e-TULA system, Nivesh Mitra integration |
| **Maharashtra** | legalmetrology.maharashtra.gov.in | Aaple Sarkar portal, GRAS payment |
| **Kerala** | lmoms.kerala.gov.in | LMOMS system |
| **Tamil Nadu** | labour.tn.gov.in | Under Labour Department |
| **Gujarat** | e-gov.gujarat.gov.in | Common service portals |

### Key Cross-State Differences
1. **Portal Technology:** Each state has independent system (PHP, .NET, React)
2. **Fee Structure:** Different fee schedules per state
3. **SLA Timelines:** Vary significantly
4. **Integration Points:** Different state e-governance platforms
5. **Document Requirements:** Additional state-specific requirements
6. **Inspection Scheduling:** Manual vs algorithmic
7. **Payment Gateways:** State-specific treasury systems
8. **Language:** Each state has own regional language support

### Common Elements (Federal)
1. **Same Act:** Legal Metrology Act, 2009
2. **Same License Categories:** Manufacturer, Dealer, Repairer
3. **Same Verification Requirements:** Section 24
4. **Same Forms:** LM-1, LD-1, LR-1 (prescribed by central rules)
5. **Same Offences & Penalties Framework:** Chapter V
